The Data Fragmentation Problem

Airfare volatility is a leading indicator of macroeconomic trends, yet traditional CPI models rely on delayed, sampled data. Fragmented carrier pricing architectures obscure true market inflation, leaving policy makers reacting rather than forecasting.
